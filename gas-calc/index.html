Capacities are transcribed from NFPA 54 Chapter 6. Natural gas: Table 6.2.1(a)–(g) Schedule 40 metallic pipe and 6.2.1(t)–(v) PE plastic pipe, stated in cubic feet per hour at SG 0.60. Undiluted propane: Table 6.3.1(a)–(d) Schedule 40 metallic and 6.3.1(k)–(l) PE, stated in thousands of BTU per hour at SG 1.50. Each run is sized against the table matching its own inlet pressure and allowable pressure drop — no multipliers or scaling factors are applied. Run length is rounded UP to the next table row, never interpolated down. CSST and TracPipe remain manufacturer data, flagged in the results — NFPA sizes CSST by EHD flow designation, not nominal size. Runs tagged as shared piping carry the generator PLUS the total connected appliance load (NFPA 54 §6.1 — no diversity factor applied); appliance load converts to CFH at 1,000 BTU/ft³. Generator demand comes from the Generac spec sheet, not from the kW rating. No run is sized below the generator gas inlet regardless of calculated capacity — set the inlet size in Step 2 from the install manual. Size for full rated load at sea level; derate for elevation per Generac spec. ANSI Z21.75 flex connector required at the generator. Lengths entered are physical run length only — add equivalent length for every elbow, tee and valve before sizing. Verify all sizing with a licensed gas fitter and the authority having jurisdiction (AHJ).
